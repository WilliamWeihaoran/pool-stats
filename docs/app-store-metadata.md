## Description

Pool Stats Tracker helps you log real match play, structure practice, and understand how your game is changing over time.

Track each rack with fast controls for break quality, layout, unforced errors, and result. Build a better practice routine with a growing drill library, adaptive difficulty, and session-level notes that stay useful after the table time ends.

The Dashboard turns your sessions into trends you can actually use: recent form, activity streaks, conversion and runout context, Fargo-inspired skill signals, and error breakdowns that help you see where racks are slipping away.

Use Goals to keep training pointed at something concrete, save opponents and shared matches, and review your history with CloudKit-backed sync across devices. The Apple Watch companion also makes it easier to quick-log sessions and stay in rhythm while you play.

Features:
- Fast rack-by-rack match logging
- Drill practice library with skill filtering and difficulty progression
- Dashboard analytics for form, activity, runout and error trends
- Goal tracking and progress review
- Match history, opponent tracking, and optional sharing features
- CloudKit sync with local offline cache
- Apple Watch companion for quick logging
- English and Simplified Chinese support

## App Review Notes Draft

Pool Stats Tracker uses CloudKit for syncing sessions, goals, and related app data across a user’s own devices. Social profile and match-sharing features are optional and user initiated.

Sign in with Apple is optional and is used only for account/profile features. The app does not require Sign in with Apple to log sessions or use CloudKit sync.

The app includes an Apple Watch companion for quick session logging.
